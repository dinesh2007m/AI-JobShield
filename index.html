Analyze Job Offer How it works

Fake job offers go after people who are eager to start

Fraudulent recruiters write convincing messages to students and job seekers. They ask for a "registration fee", a one-time password or bank details, or push the conversation onto WhatsApp or Telegram. By the time the pattern is obvious, the money or the personal data is usually gone.

JobShield gives you a second opinion in seconds, with the reasoning shown, so you can decide what to do with the offer.

What JobShield checks

17 fraud indicators

Fee and deposit requests, OTP and bank-detail requests, unrealistic pay, guaranteed jobs, pressure tactics, chat-app recruiting, suspicious email domains and more.

Explainable scoring

Each indicator adds a fixed number of points to a 0 to 100 score. You see the quoted phrase, the reason, and the points, so nothing is a black box.

Private by default

The analysis runs in your browser. Text is sent to a server only if the optional AI review is switched on for this deployment.
